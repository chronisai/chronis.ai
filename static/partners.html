Who's supporting us

Three partners,
three kinds of runway.

Chronis is built by a small team moving fast on hardware, models, and infrastructure at once. These partners give us the room to do that without slowing down to solve problems that are already someone else's expertise.

Manufacturing & Incubation

Hansraj College · MHCIIE

Manufacturing resources and incubation support

Hansraj College and its innovation and incubation cell, MHCIIE, back the physical build of the Chronis wearable. Their labs, fabrication access, and incubation framework let us move hardware iterations from prototype to production line without setting up manufacturing infrastructure from scratch.

AI Infrastructure

Sarvam AI

Compute credits and multilingual model access

Sarvam AI supports Chronis with AI credits and API access to their language models, giving us the multilingual foundation to build a memory platform that understands and responds across Indian languages rather than defaulting to English alone.

Academic Mentorship

IIT BHU

Infrastructure and mentorship through faculty advisors

IIT (BHU) Varanasi provides infrastructure support and technical guidance through a group of professors acting as mentors and advisors, helping us stress test the engineering and research decisions behind Chronis against academic rigor.

Support that compounds the work without touching who owns it.
